### Proposed section list
1. Your feed: a toy feed of coloured tiles (colour = topic) that the user clicks.
2. What it thinks of you: a live bar chart of the inferred taste profile.
3. The loop: the feed shows more of what was clicked, and a diversity-over-time line drops.
4. Fast forward: a simulated user runs 500 rounds, with an exploration-rate slider.
5. Everyone at once: a few hundred users on a 2D taste map cluster under collaborative filtering.
6. What gets amplified: an engagement-only objective lets a few high-click items take over.
7. Turning the knobs: side-by-side comparison of chronological feed, more exploration, and diversity re-ranking.
8. What the evidence says: prose on the mixed real-world research and self-selection vs. the algorithm. The sims show a mechanism, not a measured effect size.

### Proposed architecture
- One seeded, DOM-free JS sim engine (users, items, recommender, click model). Every demo is a view over it.
- Recommender: a per-topic preference bandit for sections 1–4, and small embedding-based collaborative filtering for sections 5–6.
- Headless tests for determinism per seed and for every claim the page makes (e.g. "diversity falls when exploration = 0").

### Proposed out of scope (for `docs/SCOPE.md`)
- Real platform data or scraping.
- Naming or modelling specific platforms' real algorithms.
- User accounts or tracking.
- Claims about the real-world size of the effect.
